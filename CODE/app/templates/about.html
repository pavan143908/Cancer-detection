About Project

Artificial intelligence (AI) encompasses multiple technologies with the common aim to computationally simulate human intelligence. Machine learning (ML) is a subgroup of AI that focuses on making predictions by identifying patterns in data using mathematical algorithms. Deep learning (DL) is a subgroup of ML that focuses on making predictions using multi-layered neural network algorithms inspired by the neurological architecture of the brain. Compared to other ML methods such as logistic regression, the neural network architecture of DL enables the models to scale exponentially with the growing quantity and dimensionality of data. This makes DL particularly useful for solving complex computational problems such as large-scale image classification, natural language processing and speech recognition and translation. Cancer care is undergoing a shift towards precision healthcare enabled by the increasing availability and integration of multiple data types including genomic, transcriptomic and histopathologic data. The use and interpretation of diverse and high-dimensionality data types for translational research or clinical tasks require significant time and expertise
